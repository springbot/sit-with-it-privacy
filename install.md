# Installing Sit With It on your Android phone

[מדריך בעברית](install-he)

Sit With It is a practice timer for ERP work with your therapist. It is not on Google Play yet, so you install it from a file. This takes about 3 minutes. It is safe, and you can remove it any time like any other app.

**You need:** an Android phone (Android 8 or newer) and the file `SitWithIt-0.1.0.apk`.

**Download:** https://github.com/springbot/sit-with-it-privacy/releases/latest

## Step 1: Get the file onto your phone
Open the link or message you were sent on your phone and tap the download link. If it was sent by email or WhatsApp, tap the file to download it.
If Chrome says "This type of file can harm your device", tap **Download anyway**. That is the normal warning for any app file that doesn't come from Google Play.

## Step 2: Open the file
When the download finishes, tap **Open**. You can also open the **Files** or **Downloads** app and tap `SitWithIt-0.1.0.apk`.

## Step 3: Allow the install (first time only)
Android will say it can't install apps from this source.
1. Tap **Settings**.
2. Switch on **Allow from this source**.
3. Press Back to return to the install screen.

This only applies to the app you downloaded with (for example Chrome or Files). You can switch it off again afterwards.

## Step 4: Install
Tap **Install**.
If **Play Protect** shows "App not scanned" or "Unknown developer":
1. Tap **More details**.
2. Tap **Install anyway**.

This appears because the app isn't distributed through Google Play yet.

## Step 5: Open it
Tap **Open**. When asked, tap **Allow** for notifications. The app uses them for the reminder shown during practice.

## Updating
When a new version is sent to you, install it the same way. Your data stays. Don't uninstall first.

## Removing it
Hold the app icon, tap **App info**, then **Uninstall**. This deletes your practice data from the phone.

## Your privacy
Your sessions and settings stay on your phone. The app has no account and no ads, and it doesn't send your practice data anywhere. Full policy: https://springbot.github.io/sit-with-it-privacy/

## Important
Sit With It is a practice tool to use with your therapist. It is not medical advice and is not a medical device. If you are in crisis, contact your local emergency number.

## If something goes wrong
- **"App not installed":** you may have an older copy from a different source. Uninstall it, then install again.
- **No "Install" button:** repeat Step 3 for the app you opened the file from.
- **Something else:** write to avivvro@gmail.com and say which phone you have and what you see.
