# Privacy Policy: Sit With It

Last updated: 20 November 2026
Developer: Aviv Vromen · Contact: avivvro@gmail.com

**Summary: Sit With It does not collect, transmit, or share any data. It has no internet access. Everything you enter stays on your device.**

## What the app stores
The app stores your settings, your notification variants, and your focus-session history (for example start and end times, how often the notification was dismissed, and the options you chose) in a local database on your device. This data is never sent to the developer or to anyone else.

## What the app does not do
- It does not have the Android internet permission, so it cannot send data anywhere.
- It has no analytics, advertising, crash-reporting, or tracking SDKs.
- It does not access your contacts, location, camera, microphone, or files, except a file you choose yourself when you export or back up your data (see below).
- It does not require an account.
- It has no in-app purchases.

## Permissions
- **Notifications**: to show the session notification during a session.
- **Run at startup**: to restore an active session after your device restarts.

## Exports and backups
If you export a CSV or a PDF progress summary, or back up your data, the app creates a file and hands it to the Android share sheet or to a location you choose. What happens to it afterwards depends on the app or service you share it with. Android cloud backup is disabled for this app. Your data is removed when you uninstall the app or clear its storage.

## Children
The app is not directed at children and is intended for adults (18+).

## What the app is
Sit With It is a habit and focus trainer. It does not collect health information.

## Changes
Any changes will be posted on this page and the date above will be updated.
